Rives Underground Biomes 

This mod's primary goal is to enhance caves under certain biomes, expanding them all the way down to bedrock. This merges surface and underground world together, creating seamless and unique experience.

Currently, the mod gives these biomes their own underground version:

  1. Jungle (#jungle, #is_jungle) - Underground Jungle
  2. Desert (#desert, #is_desert) - Underground Desert
  3. Cold biomes (#snowy, #is_snowy, #icy, #is_icy) - Ice Caves
  4. Coral reefs (in the warm ocean) - Underground Reef

Each underground biome features a set of these additions:

  1. Converts stone and deepslate into meaningful alternatives, including all ore variants
  2. Adds new monster or new zombie/skeleton variant
  3. Replaces vanilla dungeon room with biome-themed alternative
  4. Generates new unique structures
  5. Brings up new decorative blocks

Underground Reef:

Main Features:

  • Completely overhauled generation under warm ocean: coral reefs grow under its surface, forming multiple floors
  • Corals, seaweed, kelp, mineral formations and unique structures are scattered across all floors
  • Various ruins of ancient civilization can be found across underground reefs
  • Prismarine crystal ore forms in a pearlstone, and prismarine shards grow inside prismarine geodes
  • Tube corals connect floors, sucking water downstream or upstream

New structures:

  • Pearlstone shrines and ruins with piles of valuable materials with monster spawners inside
  • Sunken submarine - a rare structure of someone's unfortunate fate

New blocks:

  • Pearlstone - replaces all stone and deepslate
  • Pearlstone Bricks - can be used to make stairs, slabs and walls
  • Smooth Pearlstone - can be used to make stairs, slabs and walls
  • Pearlstone Sand - can be found in patches in pearlstone
  • Budding Prismarine - like budding amethyst, but grows prismarine crystals. Can be found in prismarine geodes
  • Prismarine Crystal Ore - drops prismarine, generates naturally in the lower level of underground reef

Underground Jungle:

Main Features:

  • Small and giant jungle trees grow underground
  • A mesh of jungle wood roots have grown through all biome
  • Vines, moss and grass blocks occasionaly cover surfaces
  • A lot of vegetation growing on everything it can grow on
  • Flooded areas grow seagrass, pickles, kelp

New structures:

  • Jungle Dungeon Room - replaces vanilla dungeon rooms, features a unique jungle chest
  • Giant Hives - hollow bee hives that carry honey blocks and bees inside
  • Spider Nests - a small hollow filled with leaves and cobweb, features a unique spider chest
  • Jungle Shafts - occasionally connects jungle surface with its underground version

New mobs:

  • Jungle Slime - a spiked slime, hitting it would damage you back
  • Jungle Spider - its bite inflicts nausea, spawns inside spider nests

New blocks:

  • Glowing Mushrooms
  • Mud replaces all stone. Ore generated in mud can be mined with a shovel
  • Mudstone replaces all deepslate. Mudstone can be used as a convenient source of packed mud
  • Jungle Chest - generates inside jungle dungeon rooms. Cannot be crafted

Underground Desert:

Main Features:

  • An ant burrow funnel can be found on the surface that leads to a cave openings underground
  • Giant fossils with bone blocks are common here
  • Fragile anthills are scattered on the floor. Breaking them will spawn angry ants
  • Sandstone stalactites and stalagmites
  • Dry bushes all over the place
  • Beware of quicksand pools!

New structures:

  • Desert Dungeon Room - replaces vanilla dungeon rooms, features a unique desert chest
  • Giant Fossils - various shapes of ancient creatures' skeletons made out of bone blocks and rarely embed diamonds in them
  • Prismarine Geode - a rare, blooming flooded grotto that features prismarine geode on its floor
  • Ancient Library - a rare structure
  • Ancient Observatory - a very rare structure

New mobs:

  • Ant - acts like silverfish, only spawns when breaking anthills
  • Mummy Skeleton - a desert variation of a common skeleton

New blocks:

  • Anthill - very fragile, spawns ants when breaks
  • Sandstone replaces all stone
  • Sandslate replaces all deepslate
  • Desert Chest - generates inside desert dungeon rooms and new dungeons. Cannot be crafted

Ice Caves:

Main Features:

  • Frosted caves, covered with snow, ice and icicles
  • Ice blockages with thin ice that breaks easily
  • Giant fossils with bone blocks are common here
  • Pools of frozen lakes
  • Pools of powdered snow

New structures:

  • Ice Bubbles with hot water inside are scattered around. In some you even may find a prismarine chest
  • Giant Fossils - various shapes of ancient creatures' skeletons made out of bone blocks and rarely embed diamonds in them
  • Ice Dungeon Room - replaces vanilla dungeon rooms, features a unique frost chest

New mobs:

  • Frozen - frosted variation of a common zombie
  • Strays replaces common skeletons

New blocks:

  • Permafrost replaces all stone
  • Glacislate replaces all deepslate
  • Permafrost bricks - crafted from permafrost, can be used to make stairs, slabs and walls
  • Frost crystals - grow on all surfaces, drops ice
  • Ice chest - generates inside ice dungeon rooms. Cannot be crafted
