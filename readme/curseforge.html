Rives Underground Biomes 

This mod's primary goal is to enhance caves under certain biomes, expanding them all the way down to bedrock. This merges surface and underground world together, creating seamless and unique experience.

Currently, the mod gives these biomes their own underground version:

  1. Jungle (#jungle, #is_jungle) - Underground Jungle
  2. Desert (#desert, #is_desert) - Underground Desert
  3. Cold biomes (#snowy, #is_snowy, #icy, #is_icy) - Ice Caves
  4. Coral reefs (in the warm ocean) - Underground Reef + Underground Ocean
